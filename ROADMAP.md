# KabanGo roadmap

The production directory now uses 69 public-source real business records. Next priorities: owner confirmation, broader barangay coverage beyond the city-proper OSM extract and current delivery catalog, permissioned branch-interior photo galleries, published dine-in prices, and city confirmation of fare-table ambiguities. A public-source check date is not an owner verification.

- Supabase free-tier data provider, authenticated owner dashboard, verified claims and moderated public reviews with spam controls.
- Community submission inbox and photo moderation; local draft export already works.
- Permitted offline basemap provider and explicit bounded download area, without bulk access to OSM's public tile service.
- Accurate transit stops, service availability and verified fare table updates with audit history.
- Food-aware model retrieval for larger datasets, streamed structured replies when validation can be preserved, better Hiligaynon language evaluation.
- Capacitor Android/iOS builds with native share/install behavior.
- Push notifications for opt-in promos, daily specials and a local food guide.
- Group “where to eat” voting and food-crawl route planner.
- Grounded menu summaries and richer “what should I order?” suggestions.
- Complete human-reviewed localization and accessibility/Lighthouse audits on real devices.
